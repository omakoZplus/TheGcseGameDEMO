# The Grade Grinder - A Toxic Monster's Game 💀

Welcome, foolish mortals, to **The Grade Grinder**! Dare to submit your precious GCSE grades and face the delightful torment of **Ed**, your monstrously unhelpful (and quite frankly, bored) guide through this little charade.

## What Fresh Hell is This?

You, the unfortunate user, will be prompted to divulge your so-called "personal information" – your name (as if Ed cares), your date of spawning, and the institution that failed to teach you better. Then, the real fun begins: you'll feed Ed your grades for **Maths**, **English Language**, and **English Literature**. In return, Ed will bestow upon you his... *unique* and utterly unfiltered feedback. Prepare for a rollercoaster of biting sarcasm and questionable encouragement!

## Your Charming Host - Ed

Meet Ed. He's a creature of refined taste, mostly for misery and the delightful crunch of shattered hopes. Ed finds endless amusement in the trivialities of human "achievements," especially your GCSE grades. Expect a barrage of sarcasm, cutting taunts, and a generally toxic attitude. It's all in good fun... probably. He's been described as a "delightful tormentor," but mostly he just enjoys the chaos.

## "Features" (If you can call them that)

*   **Thematic, Darkly Humorous Dialogue:** Ed's got a way with words. You'll see.
*   **Dynamic Feedback:** Your specific brand of failure (or, ugh, *success*) will determine Ed's particular flavor of mockery.
*   **Multiple "Unique" Endings:** Depending on your overall "performance," Ed has a few choice words and scenarios to send you off with. Will you be his new favorite paperweight or perhaps qualify for middle management in his corner of the abyss? Thrilling.
*   **Refactored Python Code:** Some poor soul apparently restructured the underlying incantations. Ed remains unimpressed.
*   **Unit Tests:** To ensure Ed's pronouncements of doom are consistently disheartening.

## How to Subject Yourself to This Ordeal

1.  Ensure you have a Python interpreter (if you don't, is that another failure to add to the list?).
2.  Run `main.py` from your terminal: `python main.py`
3.  Brace yourself and answer Ed's intrusive questions. Try not to cry. Or do. Ed might find it amusing.

## "Contributing" (Hah!) & Forking

Believe it or not, this "game" is open source. If you're foolish enough to think you can improve upon Ed's masterpiece of misery, feel free to fork it. Go on, try to make it "better." Or make it worse. Ed might even get a chuckle out of that. He dares you.
